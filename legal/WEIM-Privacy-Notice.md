## 2. Scope

This notice explains how we handle personal data in connection with the Whiteley Events A&H iLive Monitor desktop application (also shown in the application as "iLive Monitor", the "Software"), including registration, check-ins, update checks and related services. It supplements our website privacy notice.

## 3. What we collect

When you register and use the Software we collect and process the following personal data:

- **Your name and email address** — entered when you first use the Software, used to identify the installation and to contact you about it.
- **Computer name** — the name macOS gives the computer (System Settings → General → About), read automatically.
- **Installation identifier** — a random identifier created by the Software on first use. It is not derived from your hardware.
- **Check-in data** — sent when the Software starts and about once a day while it runs: your name, email address, computer name, installation identifier, the Software's name and version. We record when each installation last checked in. The receiving server may record the transient IP address used to make the request, for abuse prevention and rate limiting.
- **Update checks** — the Software asks whiteleyevents.co.uk for the latest version. These requests contain no personal data beyond the transient IP address described above.
- **Support data** — logs or files you choose to send us with a support request. Logs may contain channel and mix names, network addresses of your equipment and error details. Please review logs before sending and redact anything you do not wish to share.

The Software does **not** send us channel names, mix names, send levels, mixer settings or any audio. It does not use a microphone or any audio input.

### Your equipment

The Software connects directly to your MixRack over your local network. That traffic stays on your network and does not pass through us.

## 4. Why we process it and our lawful basis

We process the personal data listed above to:

- register installations and decide whether each may continue to be used (lawful basis: performance of a contract with you, UK GDPR Art. 6(1)(b));
- receive check-ins in order to detect misuse and to secure our systems (lawful basis: legitimate interests, UK GDPR Art. 6(1)(f) — protecting our business against unauthorised use);
- offer updates (lawful basis: legitimate interests in keeping the Software working and secure);
- diagnose and fix defects and respond to support enquiries (lawful basis: performance of a contract, or legitimate interests in supporting our users);
- comply with legal or regulatory obligations (lawful basis: legal obligation, UK GDPR Art. 6(1)(c)).

## 5. Who we share it with

We do not sell personal data. We share it only with:

- our hosting and email providers, acting as processors on our instructions;
- professional advisers (for example legal or accounting) where strictly necessary;
- competent authorities where we are legally required to do so.

We do not share personal data with Allen & Heath.

## 6. International transfers

Where personal data is transferred outside the UK, we rely on appropriate safeguards such as the UK International Data Transfer Agreement or the UK Addendum to the EU Standard Contractual Clauses.

## 7. Retention

- Registration records: for as long as the installation is in use and up to seven (7) years after its last check-in, to meet legal retention requirements and to support queries about it.
- Check-in records: we keep the most recent check-in time for each installation; historical check-in detail is kept for up to ninety (90) days, then deleted or fully anonymised.
- Transient request IP addresses: kept in server logs for up to thirty (30) days for abuse prevention, then deleted.
- Support correspondence and logs you send us: typically retained for up to three (3) years.

## 8. Your rights

Under UK GDPR you have the right to: access your personal data; have it corrected or erased; restrict or object to processing; data portability; and, where processing is based on consent, to withdraw that consent at any time.

To exercise these rights, contact us at hello@whiteleyevents.co.uk. We will respond within one (1) month.

You also have the right to lodge a complaint with the UK Information Commissioner's Office (ico.org.uk) if you are unhappy with how we handle your personal data. We would appreciate the chance to put things right first.

## 9. Security

We use HTTPS for all check-in and update traffic, only open update installers signed by Whiteley Events Ltd, and apply technical and organisational measures appropriate to the risk, including access controls, logging and regular review of our processors.

## 10. Changes

We may update this notice from time to time. The effective date at the top of the document indicates when it was last changed. Material changes will be communicated through the Software or via https://whiteleyevents.co.uk.
